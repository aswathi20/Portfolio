## 🚀 3-Minute Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Open Free AI Video Creator
Go to either:
- **D-ID Studio**: [https://studio.d-id.com](https://studio.d-id.com) *(Free trial credits included)*
- **HeyGen**: [https://app.heygen.com](https://app.heygen.com) *(Free trial credits included)*

### Step 2: Upload Your Photo
1. Click **Create Video**.
2. Click **+ Add** or **Upload Photo**.
3. Select your photo: `aswathi_formal_dark_studio.jpg` from your desktop or project folder.

### Step 3: Paste Your Script & Choose Voice
1. Copy **Option 1** or **Option 2** from above and paste it into the script box.
2. Select Voice:
   - **Language**: *English (India)* or *English (United States)*
   - **Voice**: Female (e.g., *Neerja*, *Swara*, or *Jenny*)
   - **Style/Tone**: Professional, friendly, confident.

### Step 4: Generate & Download
1. Click **Generate Video**.
2. Wait 30 seconds for the AI to animate your face with natural lip sync and head movements.
3. Click **Download** to save the `.mp4` file.
